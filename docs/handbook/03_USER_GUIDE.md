# 1. Welcome

Maharshi Kanad Jyotish Kendra offers Janam Kundli, Prashn Kundli, Kundli matching, Vastu, puja and other astrology services. This guide shows you how to use the website and the app: reading the Panchang, getting a free Kundli, booking a service, paying, tracking your order, and keeping your documents.

You need nothing special: a phone, tablet or computer with internet is enough. The website works in **Hindi** and **English**.

# 2. Getting around

## 2.1 Language

Use the language button in the header (HI / EN) to switch between Hindi and English at any time. The page stays where it is.

## 2.2 Menu

On a computer the menu is across the top. On a phone, press the three-line button to open it. The main places are:

| Menu | What you find |
|---|---|
| Home | Services, plans, reviews, questions and contact |
| About | Who we are and what we believe |
| Services | All consultations and reports, with prices |
| Kundli, Prashn Kundli, Matching | Free tools and paid reports |
| Booking | Book and pay for a service |
| Gallery | Photos |
| Contact | Phone, WhatsApp and a message form |

## 2.3 The four round buttons

Four small round buttons stay at the sides of the screen:

- **Call** - phone us directly.
- **WhatsApp** - start a chat with a ready message.
- **Ask AI** - get quick answers about our services.
- **Chat** - leave a message for us.

While you scroll they tuck away so they do not block the page, and they come back when you stop.

## 2.4 The date bar

The thin bar at the top shows the time, today's date, the tithi, paksha, Hindu month and Vikram Samvat. It is calculated for Bhilai and updates every day.

# 3. Free tools

## 3.1 Panchang

Open **Panchang** (from the date bar or the Tools block) to see the full daily Panchang: tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, sunrise and sunset, auspicious and inauspicious times. Choose another date or place inside the Panchang box.

## 3.2 Free Kundli

1. Open **Kundli**.
2. Enter your name, date of birth, exact time of birth and place of birth.
3. Press the button to see your chart and details.

An exact birth time gives a more correct result. If you are not sure, ask your family.

## 3.3 Kundli matching

1. Open **Kundli Matching**.
2. Enter the details of both people (boy and girl).
3. Read the Guna Milan score and the explanation.

A matching result is a guide. For a decision about marriage, please consult the astrologer in a paid consultation.

## 3.4 Request a Kundli from home

On the home page, the **Kundli request** form lets you leave your details. Fill in name, phone and what you need, press send, and we will contact you.

# 4. Booking a service

## 4.1 Choose a service and plan

1. Open **Services** and choose a service (for example Janam Kundli).
2. Read the plans. Each plan shows its price, delivery time and what is included. A plan marked "Most chosen" is the one most people pick.
3. Press **Select Plan** or **Consult Now**.

## 4.2 Sign in

You need an account to book, so we can show your order and keep your files safe.

- **Mobile OTP**: enter your mobile number and the code sent to it.
- **Email and password**: register once, then sign in with them.
- **Google**: press the Google button and choose your account.

Never share your OTP or password with anyone, including us.

## 4.3 Fill in your details

Enter your name and birth details exactly as asked. If your plan includes a call, choose a date and time from the available **slots**. Only free times are shown.

## 4.4 Promo code

If you have a code, type it in the promo box and press Apply. The discount shows before you pay. If the code is not accepted, the message tells you why (for example minimum amount, wrong service, expired, or already used).

## 4.5 Pay

You can pay by UPI or bank transfer, as shown on the payment page.

1. Pay the exact amount shown to the UPI ID, QR code or bank account given.
2. Keep the **reference number (UTR)** from your payment app.
3. Enter the UTR in the box and submit.

Your order then shows **Awaiting verification**. We check the payment and confirm it, usually within a few hours during working time. Please pay only to the details shown on our website.

# 5. After you book

## 5.1 My orders and tracking

Open your account menu, then **My Orders**. Each order shows its number, amount and status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Awaiting verification | We are checking your payment |
| Order received | Payment confirmed |
| Kundli in progress | We are preparing your report |
| Kundli complete | Your report is ready |
| Dispatched | Sent by courier (tracking number shown) |
| Delivered | Received by you |
| Execution complete | Service finished |

## 5.2 Invoice

For a paid order you can open the invoice from the order page and print or save it.

## 5.3 Your files (Vault)

Under **My Files** you can upload documents such as birth details or photos, and download your reports. PDF, pictures, video, audio and text open on screen; Word and Excel files download. Each file can be up to 4 MB. Your files are private and only you and our team can see them.

## 5.4 Your account

In **Profile** you can change your name, phone, language and password. To sign out, press **Log out**, especially on a shared device.

# 6. Associates (partners)

If you wish to earn by recommending us, you can join the associate programme.

1. Open **Become an Associate** and apply.
2. After approval you get your own referral link and code.
3. When someone books through your link or code, a commission is recorded.
4. Commission goes through stages: Pending, Earned, Paid. Earned money can be requested as a payout once it reaches the minimum amount.
5. Add your bank or UPI details in **Payout details**, then press **Request payout**. We will pay and add the reference.

Follow the programme rules; false or self-made orders can lead to cancellation.

# 7. Safety and privacy

- We never ask for your OTP, password, card PIN or bank login.
- Pay only to the UPI ID and bank details shown on the website.
- Your birth details and files are used only to prepare your service.
- Read the Privacy Policy, Terms and Refund Policy from the footer.
- If something looks wrong, call us before paying.

# 8. Questions and answers

**I did not receive my OTP.** Wait one minute and ask again. Check your number. If it still fails, sign in with email and password or Google, or call us.

**I paid but my order says Awaiting verification.** This is normal until we check the payment. If it has been a day, call or WhatsApp us with your order number and UTR.

**I made a mistake in my birth details.** Contact us before the report is complete, and we will correct it.

**Can I change or cancel my booking?** Please see the Refund Policy in the footer, and contact us as early as possible.

**The promo code is not working.** Read the message shown under the box. Codes have dates, limits and services they apply to.

**The page looks old after an update.** Refresh the page.

**Which language are the reports in?** Tell us your choice when you book.

# 9. Contact us

Phone and WhatsApp numbers are shown in the header, the footer and the Contact page, and the round Call and WhatsApp buttons reach us directly. You can also send a message from the Contact page. Please keep your order number ready when you write.
