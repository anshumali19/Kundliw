### 1. Introduction
Maharshi Kanad Jyotish Kendra ("we", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect your information when you use our website, mobile app and services.

### 2. Information We Collect
We may collect: your name, mobile number, email address, address and location details; birth details (date, time and place of birth) and other information you give for Kundli, Kundli matching, Panchang or consultation requests; files you upload (for example a Kundli or documents) to your private vault; booking, order and payment status information; messages you send to us or our chat assistant; and technical information such as device type and pages visited.
We do not store your card number, UPI PIN or bank login details. Payments are handled by our payment partner.

### 3. How We Use Your Information
To create and manage your account; to provide consultations, Kundli, reports and other services you request; to process bookings, payments, refunds and invoices; to respond to enquiries and give support; to send important service notifications (for example booking confirmation, OTP, report ready); to run the associate (referral) programme; and to improve the website and keep it secure.

### 4. Information Sharing
We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only when necessary: with the Kendra's astrologers who deliver your service; with service providers who help us run the platform (hosting, SMS/OTP, email, payments, analytics); when required by law; or to protect the rights, safety and security of the Kendra and its users. Your uploaded files are private and can be opened only by you and authorised Kendra administrators.
When you use an embedded third-party tool (for example AstroSage Panchang, Kundli or Matching), details you type into that tool are received by that provider under its own privacy policy.

### 5. Referral (Associate) Programme
If you join as an associate, we keep your referral code, commission and payout records, and the payout details (UPI/bank) you provide, only to calculate and pay commission.

### 6. Data Security and Retention
We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, misuse, alteration, loss or disclosure, including access controls and regular backups. No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We keep information as long as needed to provide services and to meet legal, accounting and dispute requirements.

### 7. Your Choices and Rights
You may ask us to correct your information, delete your account and uploaded files, or stop notifications by contacting us. Some records (such as invoices) may be kept as required by law.

### 8. User Responsibility
You are responsible for giving accurate information and for keeping your login details confidential. Do not share your OTP or password with anyone.

### 9. Cookies & Third-Party Services
Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for login, language choice and a better experience. We may use third-party services for hosting, SMS/OTP, email, payments, sign-in with Google or Facebook, an AI chat assistant, maps and analytics. These services have their own privacy policies.

### 10. Children
Our services are intended for adults. If details of a minor are submitted, it must be done by a parent or guardian.

### 11. Policy Updates
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on our website and app with a revised "Last Updated" date.
