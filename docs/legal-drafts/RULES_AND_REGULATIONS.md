# MAHARSHI KANAD JYOTISH KENDRA
## RULES & REGULATIONS
*Creating a Safe & Trusted Platform for Everyone* · DRAFT

### 1. User Guidelines
- Provide true and accurate information.
- Treat staff, astrologers and other users with respect; no harassment.
- Respect the privacy of others.
- Maintain only one account per person.
- Use genuine and appropriate details and uploads.

### 2. Communication
- Use polite and respectful language.
- Do not repeatedly contact astrologers or staff outside the booked time.
- Do not share or request OTPs, passwords or financial information in chat.
- Report suspicious, abusive or inappropriate communication.

### 3. Consultation Etiquette
- Join your booked consultation on time; late arrival may shorten the session.
- Keep your birth details ready for an accurate reading.
- Recording or sharing a consultation requires the astrologer's permission.
- Do not rush into major personal, financial or health decisions based on any single reading.

### 4. Payments and Bookings
- Pay only through the official payment screens; keep your receipt or invoice.
- Cancellation and refund rules are as stated in the Terms & Conditions.

### 5. Verification
Users may be asked to verify their mobile number, email address or identity to keep the platform safe and authentic. Associates may be asked for identity and payout verification.

### 6. Suspension & Ban
Accounts may be suspended or permanently banned for providing false information, harassment, fraud, financial solicitation, multiple accounts, inappropriate content, misuse of the referral programme, or other violations of these rules.
