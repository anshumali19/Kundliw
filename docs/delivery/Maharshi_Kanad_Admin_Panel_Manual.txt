1. ABOUT THIS MANUAL
====================

The Admin Panel lets you change everything visitors see on the website, and
everything that happens behind the scenes (bookings, payments, offers,
partners, backups), by clicking and typing. You never need to open or change
any code.

How to use this manual:

- Part 2 shows how to sign in and what the screen looks like.
- Part 3 is a quick "I want to..." table. Start there when you know the job
  but not the place.
- Part 4 is the control map: every part of the public website and the exact
  place where you change it.
- Parts 5 to 12 explain each area step by step: what you can add, edit,
  hide, reorder and delete, and what you cannot.
- Part 13 gives daily, weekly and monthly routines. Part 14 lists common
  problems.

  NOTE: Tip: nearly every text has an English box and a Hindi box. Fill
  both, so the site looks complete in both languages. If you leave a box
  empty, the standard wording is shown instead.


2. SIGNING IN AND FINDING YOUR WAY
==================================


2.1 Sign in
-----------

1. Open the website, choose Login, then "Super Admin Login".
2. Enter the admin email and password, then press the sign-in button.
3. Open the account menu and choose Admin (or add /admin to the website
  address).

Safety rules:

- Use a strong password nobody else knows, and change it now and then.
- Never share the admin password or the API keys.
- Press Log out when you finish, especially on a shared computer or phone.
- After five wrong password attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes.
  This is normal protection.


2.2 The Admin Panel layout
--------------------------

On a computer the menu is on the left. On a phone, press the "Admin menu"
button. Menu groups:

* Overview
    What it contains: Dashboard
* Bookings and money
    What it contains: Orders, Services and plans, Other services,
    Appointment slots, Promo codes, Enquiries
* People
    What it contains: Users, Associates, Customer files
* Website
    What it contains: Website settings (everything on the public site),
    Banners, Scrolling notice, Pages and legal, Articles, Gallery, Reviews,
    FAQ, Founder, Certificates, Translations, Chatbot
* System
    What it contains: Business settings, API keys and Analytics, Backups

The dark strip at the top shows who is signed in, a link to the public website
("Public Site") and the Log out button.


2.3 How saving works
--------------------

- Every screen has a Save button. Nothing changes on the website until you
  press it.
- After saving, the website changes at once. If you still see the old
  version, refresh the page (Ctrl and F5).
- A green message confirms a save. A red message explains what to correct.
  Nothing is lost when you see a red message.
- Items can usually be switched off (hidden) instead of deleted. Hide first;
  delete only when you are sure.

5. HOME PAGE: STEP BY STEP
==========================

All home page controls are inside Website settings. The left list is grouped;
the first group is "Home page".


5.1 Banner (hero)
-----------------

1. Open Website settings, then "Home page: banner (hero)".
2. Banner picture 1 and picture 2: press Upload and choose a photo
  (landscape pictures look best), or paste a picture address. Picture 2 fades
  in and out slowly.
3. Darkness over the pictures: 0 shows the picture as it is, 90 makes it
  very dark. Around 60 keeps white text easy to read.
4. First button text and where it goes: for example "Book Your Consultation"
  going to /services. The place can be a page of the site (/services), a full
  web address (https://...), a phone link (tel:+919827991224) or a mail link.
5. Second button: same. Switch off "Show the second button" to remove it.
6. Press Save.

The small heading, big heading and the paragraph of the banner are in Website
settings, "Hero content". Leave a box empty to use the standard wording. The
paragraph accepts links (see part 8).


5.2 Show or hide sections
-------------------------

Website settings, "Home page: show or hide sections". Each block of the home
page has its own switch: three cards, About, services grid, plans, connected
services, free tools, request form, gallery, booking steps, payment, reviews,
FAQ and contact. Hidden blocks are saved and can be switched on again at any
time.


5.3 Headings and sentences
--------------------------

Four screens hold the wording of the home page. In each, every box has an
English and a Hindi version. Boxes that show an Add link button accept links
and bold words.

* Home page: three cards and About
    What it controls: The three cards' titles and texts; the About small
    heading, heading, text, picture, three points and button (the button can
    go to any link; empty means WhatsApp)
* Home page: services, plans and tools headings
    What it controls: Headings and texts above the services, the three plan
    sections, connected services and free tools; the "Consult Now" and
    "Select Plan" words
* Home page: request form, gallery, booking and payment
    What it controls: Request form heading and side panel; gallery heading;
    booking heading, the four steps and the button; payment heading, the
    left box steps (one per line) and the right box text
* Home page: reviews, FAQ and contact block
    What it controls: Headings for reviews and FAQ; contact heading, text,
    the "How we can help" box with three points, and the button

How to change a sentence: open the screen, click the box, type, press Save. To
go back to the standard wording, clear the box and save.


5.4 Highlight strip
-------------------

Website settings, "Home page". The "Highlight strip" in English and Hindi
shows a one-line message under the banner (for example an offer or a festival
notice). Leave both empty to hide it. It accepts links.


6. MENUS, LINKS AND BUTTONS
===========================


6.1 Main menu
-------------

Website settings, "Main menu links".

1. Press Add link. Type the name in English and Hindi, and where it goes
  (for example /services, https://example.com or tel:+91...).
2. Use the Show tick to hide a link without deleting it.
3. Use the up and down arrows to change the order. Use the cross to remove a
  link.
4. Press Save.

If the list is empty, the standard menu (Home, About, Services, Kundli, Prashn
Kundli, Matching, Booking, Gallery, Contact) is used. "Go back to the standard
menu" clears your list.


6.2 Extra footer links
----------------------

"Extra footer links" works the same way. The links appear in a "More links"
row above the copyright line. Good for a WhatsApp group, a YouTube video or
another website.


6.3 Social media
----------------

1. Open "Social media". Each platform has a row with a name, a link and
  Active or Hide.
2. Paste the full address of your page (for example
  https://www.instagram.com/yourpage). For WhatsApp you can type the mobile
  number.
3. Choose Active to show it, Hide to keep it off.
4. Use the small arrow to test the link, then Save.

Active icons appear in the top bar, the home banner, the Contact page and the
footer. No keys are needed.


6.4 Top bar
-----------

"Top bar" has a switch for every part: time, seconds, day, date, paksha, tithi
(with end time), Hindu month, Vikram Samvat, Panchang Calendar button, phone,
social icons, Sign in and Register links, and the whole bar. The Panchang
values are calculated automatically every day.


6.5 Floating buttons
--------------------

"Floating buttons" controls the four round buttons:

- Switch Call, WhatsApp, Ask AI and Chat on or off one by one, and the "Ask
  a question" bar.
- Number the call button dials and WhatsApp number: leave empty to use the
  main phone from Business settings.
- First WhatsApp message: the sentence that is already typed when a visitor
  opens WhatsApp (English and Hindi).

8. WRITING TEXT WITH LINKS (HYPERLINKS)
=======================================

Every large text box in the Admin Panel has an Add link button. This is how to
use it:

1. Type your sentence, for example: Call us today for a consultation.
2. Select the words that should be clickable (for example "Call us today")
  with the mouse.
3. Press Add link. A small box opens with the selected words filled in.
4. In "Where it goes" type the address:
- another page of your site: /services or /contact
- a website: https://www.example.com
- a phone number: tel:+919827991224
- an email: mailto:name@example.com
5. Press Insert link. In the box it appears as [Call us
  today](tel:+919827991224). That is normal; visitors see a clickable link.

More ways to format:

* [Our YouTube channel](https://youtube.com/@name)
    Visitors see: a clickable link
* important
    Visitors see: bold text
* a blank line
    Visitors see: a new paragraph

For safety, only web, phone, mail and in-site addresses become links. Anything
else stays as plain text.

You can also add link boxes, pictures and videos inside Services, Pages,
Articles, Founder and similar screens using "content blocks": add a text,
image, YouTube or link block and move it up or down.


9. CONTENT SCREENS
==================


9.1 Services and plans
----------------------

Menu: Bookings and money, Services and plans.

A service is what you offer (Janam Kundli). A plan is a version with its own
price (Basic, Detailed, Premium).

Add a service: press Add Service; enter the title, tagline and description in
English and Hindi; add a picture or a YouTube link; add content blocks and
extra links; switch Active on; Save.

Add a plan: click the service, press Add Plan; enter title (English, Hindi),
the price in rupees (for example 501), optional original price, delivery days,
pages, the list of features (one per line), "Most chosen" if it should stand
out, and tick "includes call" with minutes if a consultation call is part of
the plan. A call plan needs appointment slots (see 10.2).

Editing and removing: Edit changes any detail; prices of earlier orders do not
change. Deactivate hides a service or plan and keeps its history. Delete
removes it for ever; a service that already has orders should be deactivated.

The home page uses the services named janam-kundli, prashna-kundli and
kundli-matching for its three plan sections. Other active services still
appear on the Services page.


9.2 Other services
------------------

Puja, Paath, Yagya, Vastu, Vehicle, Driver, ITR help desk and similar. For

Connected Services boxes), order and Active.


9.3 Banners

Pictures with a title and link at the top, bottom, left or right. Choose

9.4 Scrolling notice (marquee)

The moving strip at the bottom (or top). Set the text in both languages,

9.5 Pages and legal

Edit About and the legal pages (Privacy Policy, Terms and Conditions, Refund

9.6 Articles, Gallery, Reviews, FAQ, Founder

- Articles (blog): title, short summary, picture, text (accepts links),

9.7 Certificates

Upload GST, MSME, Trademark, ISO and other certificates so visitors can view,

9.8 Translations and Chatbot

- Chatbot: add question-and-answer pairs with keywords; edit the welcome

10. BOOKINGS AND MONEY
======================


10.1 Orders
-----------

Each booking becomes an order (for example MKJK-2026-0004).

Find an order: use the search box (number, name or phone) and the tabs (All,
Awaiting verification, Paid, In progress, Completed).

Verify a payment. Customers pay to your UPI or bank and enter the reference
(UTR). Such an order says "Awaiting verification".

1. Check your bank or UPI statement for that amount and reference.
2. Open the order. Compare amount and reference.
3. Press Approve if the money has arrived (the order becomes Paid and an
  invoice number is made), or Reject with a clear reason if not.

  NOTE: Never approve a payment only because the customer says they paid.

Move the order forward. Change the status so the customer can follow progress.
A short note is required each time. You cannot skip steps.

* Order received
    Meaning: Booking made
    Next: Kundli in progress
* Kundli in progress
    Meaning: Work started
    Next: Kundli complete
* Kundli complete
    Meaning: Report ready
    Next: Dispatched (physical) or Execution complete (digital)
* Dispatched
    Meaning: Sent by courier
    Next: Delivered
* Delivered
    Meaning: Customer has it
    Next: Execution complete

For dispatched orders enter the courier name and tracking number. Appointments
(for call plans) can be approved, rejected or completed from the order.
Invoices of paid orders can be opened from the order.


10.2 Appointment slots
----------------------

Slots are the times customers can choose for call plans. Without slots, call
plans cannot be booked.

1. Choose From date and To date, and tick the weekdays you are available.
2. Enter the daily Start and End time, the minutes per slot (for example 30)
  and people per slot (usually 1).
3. The screen tells you how many slots it will create. Press Add slots.

Slots already existing for the same day and time are skipped; past dates are
ignored. In the list, "Switch off" hides a slot (use for holidays); Delete
removes an unused slot. A slot with bookings cannot be deleted.


10.3 Promo codes
----------------

1. Press Create Offer Code. Enter the code (letters and numbers), titles in
  both languages, discount type (flat rupees or percentage) and value.
2. Optional: maximum discount (percentage codes), minimum order, total uses,
  uses per customer (1 is usual, 0 unlimited), the services it works on (none
  ticked means all), start and end dates.
3. Keep Active on and Save.

If a code does not apply, customers see exactly why (minimum amount, wrong
service, already used, expired or full). Make one code per campaign and switch
it off when it ends.


10.4 Enquiries
--------------

Messages from the Contact page, the home page Kundli request form and the chat
appear here with name, phone and message. Call or message the person, then
press the resolved toggle. Filters show open, resolved or all.


11. PEOPLE
==========

- Users: all registered customers with phone, email and number of orders;
  use search to help someone find their account.
- Associates: four tabs. *Associates* (approve, reject, view details, notes
  and own commission), *Commission rules* (standard percentage or amount, for
  everything or one service or plan), *Payout requests* (mark Paid with
  reference, or Rejected with reason) and *Settings* (programme on or off,
  automatic approval, minimum payout, referral days, terms). Commission
  stages: Pending, Earned, Paid, Cancelled. Never mark a payout Paid before
  the money is sent.
- Customer files: open any customer's uploaded files and upload a report for
  them. PDF, pictures, video, audio and text open on screen; Word and Excel
  download. Maximum 4 MB per file. Files that pretend to be another type are
  refused automatically.


12. SYSTEM
==========


12.1 Business settings
----------------------

Contact and name, Panchang coordinates, the map (whether to show it, where,
label), the app download link, manual payment options (UPI ID, QR picture,
bank details) and GST/billing. The email and the address are empty until you
enter them; once entered they appear on the Contact page, footer and invoices.
Double-check the UPI and bank details: customers pay to exactly what is
written.


12.2 SEO and Google
-------------------

Website settings, SEO: page title and description for Google (English and
Hindi), keywords, the picture shown when someone shares the link, and the
Google Search Console code. Tips:

- Write a title under 70 characters and a description under 160 that include
  words people search for, such as "Janam Kundli", "Kundli matching", "Prashn
  Kundli", "Vastu" and your service names.
- After the site is on your own domain, add it to Google Search Console and
  submit the sitemap address (your website address followed by /sitemap.xml).
- Keep service descriptions, FAQ answers and article texts detailed and
  honest; Google reads them.
- The site already adds search-friendly data automatically: page titles,
  descriptions, language versions, a sitemap, and FAQ and service information.


12.3 API keys and Analytics
---------------------------

Stores the connections to outside services (encrypted): Razorpay, MSG91, email
(SMTP), Google login and the Google Analytics ID. Enter Analytics like this:
create a Web property at analytics.google.com, copy the Measurement ID (starts
with G-), paste it here and save, then check Realtime in Google Analytics. To
stop tracking use Website settings, Analytics.

  NOTE: Never send these keys by WhatsApp or email in plain text.


12.4 Backups
------------

An automatic copy is made every day (plus weekly on Sundays and monthly on the
1st). Choose how many copies to keep. "Back up now" takes a copy at once (do
it before big changes). Download saves a readable copy to your computer (keep
one monthly). Passwords and secret keys are never included, and uploaded files
are stored separately. Restoring is done by the developer.


13. ROUTINES
============

Every day: open the Dashboard and clear "Payments to verify"; reply to
Enquiries; update order statuses.

Every week: add appointment slots for the next weeks; review associate
requests and payouts; check that banners, the highlight strip and the
scrolling notice are current.

Every month: download a backup; switch off finished promo codes; look at
Google Analytics; check certificates and legal pages.


14. COMMON PROBLEMS
===================

* Customer says they paid but the order is still "Awaiting verification"
    What to do: Check your bank or UPI statement for amount and reference,
    then Approve or Reject
* A promo code is refused
    What to do: Read the message shown; check minimum order, services, dates
    and per-customer limit
* Customers cannot choose a consultation time
    What to do: Add slots in Appointment slots
* I saved but the website shows the old text
    What to do: Refresh with Ctrl and F5; check you pressed Save and saw the
    green message
* A link I typed does not work
    What to do: Links need https:// at the start, or / for a page of the
    site; use the Add link button
* The menu looks wrong after my changes
    What to do: Website settings, Main menu links, press "Go back to the
    standard menu"
* White text is hard to read after changing colours
    What to do: Pick a darker "Dark brown" or press "Use standard colour"
* A Kundli, Matching or Panchang page is empty
    What to do: The outside tool may be down; try later or switch the page
    off
* The map is not shown
    What to do: Business settings, Location Map: switch it on and enter the
    place
* I forgot my password
    What to do: Contact the developer
* I deleted something by mistake
    What to do: Contact the developer at once; downloaded backups help
* Mobile OTP is not arriving
    What to do: MSG91 must be set up (keys and template); meanwhile use
    email and password or Google


15. DO AND DO NOT
=================

Do: check payments in your own bank before approving; fill Hindi and English;
keep slots and notices current; download a monthly backup; log out when
finished.

Do not: share the admin password or keys; approve a payment without checking
the money; delete services that have orders (deactivate them); change legal
text without the lawyer; upload very large pictures; make the Dark brown
colour light.

Contact the developer for restores, new features or anything unclear. Please
