1. ABOUT THIS MANUAL
====================

This manual explains, step by step, how to use the Admin Panel of the Maharshi
Kanad Jyotish Kendra website. You do not need to know any programming.
Everything below is done by clicking and typing inside the Admin Panel.

How to read it:

- Part 2 shows how to sign in and find your way around.
- Part 3 is a quick "I want to..." list. Use it when you know what you want
  to do but not where to find it.
- Parts 4 to 9 describe every screen in the order of the left-hand menu.
- Part 10 lists daily, weekly and monthly routines.
- Part 11 explains common problems and what to do.

  NOTE: Tip: Most screens show Hindi and English side by side. Always fill
  both languages for text that customers see, so the website looks complete
  in both.


2. SIGNING IN AND FINDING YOUR WAY
==================================


2.1 Signing in
--------------

1. Open the website and choose Login, then "Super Admin Login".
2. Enter the admin email and password.
3. After signing in, click Admin in the account menu (or open the address
  /admin).

Rules for safety:

- Use a strong password that nobody else knows. Change it from time to time.
- Never share the admin password. Give staff their own access only if the
  developer sets it up.
- Always press "Log out" when you finish, especially on a shared computer or
  phone.
- After five wrong password attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes.
  This is normal protection.


2.2 The Admin Panel layout
--------------------------

On a computer, the menu is on the left. On a phone, press the "Admin menu"
button to open it. The menu has five groups:

* Overview
    What it contains: Dashboard
* Bookings and money
    What it contains: Orders, Services and plans, Other services,
    Appointment slots, Promo codes, Enquiries
* People
    What it contains: Users, Associates, Customer files
* Website
    What it contains: Website settings, Banners, Scrolling notice, Pages and
    legal, Articles, Gallery, Reviews, FAQ, Founder, Certificates,
    Translations, Chatbot
* System
    What it contains: Business settings, API keys and Analytics, Backups

The black strip at the top shows who is signed in, a link to the public
website ("Public Site") and the Log out button.

4. BOOKINGS AND MONEY
=====================


4.1 Dashboard
-------------

The Dashboard is the first screen. It shows, at a glance:

- Total orders, payments waiting to be verified, revenue (paid orders only),
  customers, associates, open enquiries and active services.
- Recent orders with their payment status.
- Orders grouped by stage (received, in progress, complete and so on).

Use it every morning. If "Payments to verify" is not zero, open Orders and
verify them.


4.2 Orders
----------

Menu: Bookings and money, Orders.

This is the most important screen. Each booking becomes an order with a number
such as MKJK-2026-0004.


FINDING AN ORDER:

- Use the search box (order number, customer name or phone number).
- Use the filter tabs: All orders, Awaiting verification, Paid, In progress,
  Completed.


VERIFYING A MANUAL PAYMENT:

Customers pay to the Kendra's UPI or bank account and then enter the payment
reference (UTR) on the website. Such an order shows "Awaiting verification".

1. Open your bank or UPI app and check that the money has arrived for that
  amount.
2. In Orders, click the order.
3. Compare the amount and the reference number the customer entered.
4. Press Approve if the money has arrived. The order becomes Paid and an
  invoice number is created.
5. Press Reject only if the money has not arrived or the reference is wrong,
  and write a clear reason. The customer will see the reason and can submit
  again.

  NOTE: Important: Never approve a payment only because the customer says
  they paid. Always check your own bank or UPI statement first.


MOVING THE ORDER FORWARD:

After payment, update the status so the customer can follow progress in "Track
order". A short note is required with each change.

* Order received
    Meaning: Booking made
    Next step allowed: Kundli in progress
* Kundli in progress
    Meaning: Work has started
    Next step allowed: Kundli complete
* Kundli complete
    Meaning: Report is ready
    Next step allowed: Dispatched (physical copy) or Execution complete
    (digital only)
* Dispatched
    Meaning: Sent by courier
    Next step allowed: Delivered
* Delivered
    Meaning: Customer has it
    Next step allowed: Execution complete
* Execution complete
    Meaning: Finished
    Next step allowed: none

For a dispatched order, enter the courier name and tracking number. The
customer sees them on the tracking page.

You cannot skip steps. This keeps the record honest.


APPOINTMENTS:

If a plan includes a consultation call, the order shows the chosen date and
time. Approve, reject or mark the appointment complete from the order.
Rejecting an appointment frees that time for other customers.


INVOICES:

Paid orders have an invoice that the customer can download from their account.
You can open the same invoice from the order.


4.3 Services and plans
----------------------

Menu: Bookings and money, Services and plans.

A Service is what you offer (for example "Complete Janampatri"). A Plan is a
version of it with its own price (for example "Digital PDF" or "Handcrafted
Pothi").


ADD A SERVICE:

1. Press Add Service.
2. Enter the title in English and in Hindi, a short summary and the full
  description. Use content blocks (text, image, YouTube video, link) instead
  of one long paragraph.
3. Add a picture or a YouTube link if you have one.
4. Keep "Active" switched on to show it on the website. Switch it off to
  hide it without deleting.
5. Save.


ADD A PLAN TO A SERVICE:

1. Click the service row to open its plans.
2. Press Add Plan.
3. Enter the title (English and Hindi) and the price in rupees (for example
  1100, not 1,100.00).
4. Optionally enter the "original price" to show a cut-off price.
5. Enter delivery days and number of pages.
6. Tick "includes call" if a consultation call is part of the plan, and
  enter the call minutes. Such plans ask the customer to choose an appointment
  slot, so make sure slots exist (section 4.5).
7. Save.


EDITING AND REMOVING:

- Edit changes any detail. Prices of past orders do not change.
- Deactivate hides a service or plan without losing its history.
- Delete removes it for good. A service with orders should be deactivated
  instead of deleted.


4.4 Other services
------------------

Menu: Bookings and money, Other services.

Use this for additional services (for example Vastu, gemstones, pooja) that
are shown on the "Other services" page. It works like Services: title,
description, image or YouTube link, content blocks, and an Active switch.


4.5 Appointment slots
---------------------

Menu: Bookings and money, Appointment slots.

Slots are the times a customer can pick for a consultation call. Without
slots, customers cannot book plans that include a call.


ADDING SLOTS IN BULK:

1. Choose From date and To date.
2. Tick the weekdays you are available.
3. Enter the daily Start time and End time (for example 11:00 to 13:00).
4. Enter the minutes per slot (for example 30). The screen splits the time
  automatically: 11:00-13:00 with 30 minutes gives four slots a day.
5. Enter "People per slot" (usually 1).
6. The screen tells you how many slots it will create. Press Add slots.

Slots that already exist for the same day and start time are skipped. Past
dates are ignored.


MANAGING SLOTS:

- The list shows each date with "booked of capacity".
- Switch off hides a slot from customers without deleting it (use this for
  holidays).
- Delete removes an unused slot. A slot with bookings cannot be deleted;
  switch it off instead.

  NOTE: Habit: add slots for the next two or three weeks every Monday.


4.6 Promo codes
---------------

Menu: Bookings and money, Promo codes.

A promo code gives a customer a discount at booking.


CREATING A CODE:

1. Press Create Offer Code.
2. Code: letters and numbers only, for example NAVRATRI200.
3. Title in English and Hindi (shown to customers).
4. Discount type: Flat (a fixed rupee amount) or Percentage.
5. Value: the rupees off, or the percent.
6. Maximum discount (percentage codes only): the highest rupee amount the
  discount can reach.
7. Minimum order: the plan price must be at least this much. Leave at 0 for
  no minimum. Remember that plan prices are often around Rs. 1,100 to Rs.
  2,500, so a high minimum will block most customers.
8. Total uses: how many times the code may be used in all.
9. Uses per customer: how many times one customer may use it (1 is usual; 0
  means unlimited).
10. Valid for: tick the services it works on. Tick none for all services.
11. Start and end dates, and the Active switch.


WHAT THE CUSTOMER SEES:

If a code does not apply, the website says exactly why: "Minimum order value
of Rs. X required", "This code is not valid for the selected service", "You
have already used this code", or that it has expired or reached its limit.


GOOD PRACTICE:

- Make one code per campaign so you can see how well each worked (the list
  shows how many times it has been used).
- Switch a code off (do not delete) when a campaign ends.


4.7 Enquiries
-------------

Menu: Bookings and money, Enquiries.

Messages sent from the Contact page and the chat assistant appear here with
the person's name, phone and message. Call or message them, then press the
resolved toggle so the dashboard count goes down. Use the filter to see open,
resolved or all enquiries. Check this screen at least twice a day.


5. PEOPLE
=========


5.1 Users
---------

Menu: People, Users.

Lists all registered customers with phone, email and number of orders. Use
search to find a person. Use this to help a customer who cannot find their
account.


5.2 Associates (referral partners)
----------------------------------

Menu: People, Associates.

Associates are partners who share a referral link. When someone books through
their link, the associate earns a commission.


THE FOUR TABS:

The Associates screen has four tabs:

* Associates
    What it is for: The list of partners; approve, reject, view details,
    notes and own commission
* Commission rules
    What it is for: The standard commission, either for everything or for a
    chosen service or plan (a percentage or a fixed rupee amount)
* Payout requests
    What it is for: Requests from associates to be paid; mark each as Paid
    or Rejected
* Settings
    What it is for: The programme rules below


PROGRAMME RULES (SETTINGS TAB):

* Programme on or off
    Meaning: Turn off to stop new associate sign-ups
* Approve automatically
    Meaning: If off, you approve each new associate by hand
* Minimum payout
    Meaning: The least amount an associate may request
* Referral validity (days)
    Meaning: How long a referral link is remembered
* Terms (English and Hindi)
    Meaning: Rules shown to associates


APPROVING AN ASSOCIATE:

1. Open Associates. New requests show "Pending".
2. Open the associate. Check their name, phone, email and payout details.
3. Press Approve, or Reject with a reason.


SPECIAL ARRANGEMENTS:

For one associate you can set their own commission (percentage or fixed),
write private notes, or switch them off. A commission cannot exceed 100
percent.


COMMISSION LIFE CYCLE:

* Pending
    Meaning: The referred order exists but payment is not yet verified
* Earned
    Meaning: The order is paid, so the commission is due
* Paid
    Meaning: You have paid the associate
* Cancelled
    Meaning: The order was refunded or cancelled


PAYING COMMISSION:

1. Associates request a payout when their earned amount reaches the minimum.
2. Open the Payout requests tab. Check the associate's UPI or bank details.
3. Pay them from your bank or UPI app.
4. Mark the request as Paid and enter the payment reference. Use Rejected,
  with a reason, if you cannot pay it.

  NOTE: Never mark a payout as Paid before the money has actually been sent.


5.3 Customer files (vault)
--------------------------

Menu: People, Customer files.

Customers upload their Kundli and documents. Here you can open any customer's
files, and upload a report for them (for example the finished Kundli PDF).
Files are private: only that customer and the admin can open them.

- Accepted types: PDF, images, video, audio, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text.
  Maximum size 4 MB per file.
- PDFs, images, video, audio and text open on screen. Word and Excel files
  download.
- Pictures that look like another type (for example a program renamed as
  .pdf) are refused automatically.


6. WEBSITE
==========


6.1 Website settings
--------------------

Menu: Website, Website settings.

This is the control centre for the public website. Pick a section on the left
and save when done. Changes appear on the website immediately.

* Logo
    What it controls: Logo image and browser tab icon. Upload an image, then
    Save. Leave empty to keep the default
* Home page
    What it controls: The highlight strip under the top banner, and whether
    the social media row shows on the home page
* General
    What it controls: Tagline, footer note and copyright line
* Contact
    What it controls: Working hours and a note shown on the Contact page
* Social media
    What it controls: Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp, Twitter/X,
    LinkedIn, Telegram (see below)
* SEO
    What it controls: The title and description Google shows, keywords, the
    picture shown when the link is shared, and the Google Search Console
    code
* Content
    What it controls: Short "About" text and mission text
* Features
    What it controls: Show or hide the floating buttons, the certificates
    link and the blog link
* Business
    What it controls: Registered business name, owner, MSME number, year
    started (shown in the footer)
* Analytics
    What it controls: Allow or stop visitor statistics
* Legal pages
    What it controls: A short disclaimer in the footer, and a button to open
    Pages
* Hero animation
    What it controls: The large Om mark, a soft glow and the colour of the
    small heading in the home page banner
* Hero content
    What it controls: The small heading, main heading and introduction on
    the home page. Leave empty for the standard wording
* Technology information
    What it controls: A note on the calculation method shown under the
    Kundli, Matching and Panchang tools
* Mobile app
    What it controls: Play Store, App Store and APK links and text shown on
    the App page
* Panchang, Kundli and Matching tools
    What it controls: Switch each page on or off and change the tool address
* Admin settings
    What it controls: Private notes for staff (not shown to visitors)


SOCIAL MEDIA (STEP BY STEP):

1. Open Website settings and choose Social media.
2. For each platform, check the name and paste the full web address (for
  example https://www.instagram.com/yourpage). For WhatsApp you can type the
  mobile number; it becomes a link.
3. Set the status to Active to show it or Hide to keep it off the website.
4. Use the small arrow icon to test the link.
5. Press Save social links.

Active links appear as round icons in the top bar, the home page, the Contact
page and the footer. No technical keys are needed for these links.


SWITCHING KUNDLI, MATCHING OR PANCHANG OFF:

Choose "Panchang, Kundli and Matching tools". Untick a page and Save. The
menu, footer and home links disappear, and anyone who opens the page sees a
polite "not available right now" message. Tick it again to bring it back.


THE DATE AND PANCHANG BAR:

The top line of the website shows the time, day, date, paksha, tithi, lunar
month and Vikram Samvat. It is calculated automatically every day. There is
nothing to update.


6.2 Banners
-----------

Menu: Website, Banners.

Banners are pictures with a title and link shown at the top, bottom, left or
right of the home page.

1. Press Add Banner.
2. Choose the position (Top, Bottom, Left, Right) and the type (Single, or
  Carousel for a slideshow).
3. Upload an image or paste an address, add the titles in both languages,
  and a link if needed.
4. Set the order and keep Active on.

Use clear, light images. Very large files make the page slow; prefer files
under 500 KB.


6.3 Scrolling notice (marquee)
------------------------------

Menu: Website, Scrolling notice.

The moving text strip. You can set:

- The text in Hindi and English.
- Where it shows (top or bottom of the page).
- Speed and font size.
- Background colour and text colour. Quick colour buttons are provided
  (Yellow, Saffron, Maroon, Red, Cream). Choose a dark text on a light
  background or the reverse so it is easy to read.
- Pause when the pointer is over it, and whether visitors may close it with
  the X button.

A live preview is shown before you save.


6.4 Pages and legal
-------------------

Menu: Website, Pages and legal.

Edit pages such as About and the legal pages (Privacy Policy, Terms and
Conditions, Refund policy, Platform Guidelines, Member Agreement, Rules and
Regulations). Each page has an English text and a Hindi text, and can have
content blocks.

  NOTE: Legal wording should be changed only with the approval of the
  Kendra's lawyer.


6.5 Articles
------------

Menu: Website, Articles. Write blog articles with a title, summary, image,
text blocks and an optional YouTube video. Use the Active switch to publish or
hide an article.


6.6 Gallery
-----------

Menu: Website, Gallery. Add photos or videos with captions. Remove old items
to keep the page quick.


6.7 Reviews
-----------

Menu: Website, Reviews. Add customer testimonials with name, place, rating and
text. Only add genuine reviews with the customer's permission.


6.8 FAQ and Founder
-------------------

- FAQ: add questions and answers in both languages. Keep answers short.
- Founder: edit the founder's name, photo, biography and achievements shown
  on the Founder page.


6.9 Certificates
----------------

Menu: Website, Certificates.

Upload GST, MSME, Trademark, ISO and other certificates so visitors can view,
download and print them.

1. Press Add document.
2. Choose the kind (GST, MSME, Trademark, ISO, Other).
3. Enter the title in both languages and the certificate number.
4. Upload the PDF or image.
5. Save. Use the Active switch to hide a certificate without deleting.


6.10 Translations
-----------------

Menu: Website, Translations. Change the wording of fixed website text
(buttons, labels) in Hindi or English without any programming. Search for the
text and edit it.


6.11 Chatbot
------------

Menu: Website, Chatbot.

The chat assistant answers questions using the question-and-answer pairs you
add here, and an AI helper for other questions.

- Add common questions with keywords, answers in both languages and a short
  button text.
- Edit the welcome message.
- Switch the chatbot on or off.

  NOTE: The AI answers are general. Add your own answers for prices, timings
  and policies so customers always get correct information.

These are not edited in the panel but you should know what they do:

8. SYSTEM
=========


8.1 Business settings
---------------------

Menu: System, Business settings.

* Kendra contact and address
    What to enter: Name, phone numbers, email, address, city
* Panchang coordinates
    What to enter: Latitude and longitude used for sunrise and similar
    timings (Varanasi is set by default)
* Location map
    What to enter: Whether to show a map, its zoom, the place (latitude and
    longitude, or paste a Google Maps embed code) and the label
* Android app download
    What to enter: The link to the app and a QR code is made automatically
* Manual payment options
    What to enter: UPI ID, QR code image, bank name, account name, account
    number and IFSC. These appear at checkout
* Taxation and billing
    What to enter: GSTIN and the GST rate printed on invoices

Double-check the UPI ID and bank details. Customers will send money to exactly
what is written here.


CHANGING THE MAP LOCATION:

1. Business settings, Location Map.
2. Either type the latitude and longitude of your place, or paste the "Embed
  a map" code from Google Maps (Share, Embed a map).
3. Press Save. The Contact page and footer update immediately.


8.2 API keys and Analytics
--------------------------

Menu: System, API keys and Analytics.

This screen stores the connections to outside services. Secret values are
stored encrypted and are shown only as hidden characters.

* Razorpay Key ID, Key Secret, Webhook Secret
    Used for: Online payment
    Where to get it: Razorpay dashboard
* MSG91 Auth Key, Sender ID, Template ID
    Used for: OTP by SMS
    Where to get it: MSG91 account
* SMTP host, port, user, password, from
    Used for: Order emails
    Where to get it: Gmail App Password or another mail service
* Google Client ID and Secret
    Used for: Sign in with Google
    Where to get it: Google Cloud Console
* Google Analytics Measurement ID
    Used for: Visitor statistics
    Where to get it: analytics.google.com

Entering Google Analytics:

1. In Google Analytics, create a Web property and copy the Measurement ID
  (it starts with G-).
2. Paste it into "Google Analytics" on this screen and save.
3. Open the website, then check Google Analytics, Realtime. You should see
  yourself within a minute.

To stop tracking at any time, use Website settings, Analytics.

  NOTE: Never send these keys on WhatsApp or email in plain text. Enter them
  here yourself, or give them to the developer in person.


8.3 Backups
-----------

Menu: System, Backups.

The website keeps copies of your content and records (services, plans, orders,
customers' details, settings, certificates and so on). Passwords and secret
keys are never included. Uploaded files are stored separately and are not
inside the backup.

- Automatic backups: one daily copy, a weekly copy on Sundays and a monthly
  copy on the 1st (Indian time).
- You choose how many daily, weekly and monthly copies to keep. Older ones
  are removed automatically.
- Back up now: takes a manual copy immediately. Do this before any large
  change (for example editing many prices).
- Download: saves a copy to your computer as a readable file. Keep a monthly
  copy on your own computer or in cloud storage.
- Restore: if something goes wrong, contact the developer. Restoring is done
  carefully by the developer so that nothing is overwritten by mistake.


9. THE CUSTOMER'S JOURNEY (WHAT HAPPENS ON THE OTHER SIDE)
==========================================================

1. The customer opens a service, chooses a plan, and presses Book.
2. They sign in (OTP, password or Google), fill birth details and, for call
  plans, pick a slot.
3. They apply a promo code if they have one and see the price with GST.
4. They pay to the Kendra's UPI or bank and enter the payment reference.
5. You verify the payment (4.2). The customer gets an invoice.
6. You move the order through the stages and upload the report to their
  vault.
7. The customer tracks progress, downloads the report and the invoice.


10. ROUTINES
============


EVERY DAY:

- Open the Dashboard. Clear "Payments to verify".
- Check Enquiries and reply.
- Update order statuses for work done.


EVERY WEEK:

- Add appointment slots for the coming weeks (4.5).
- Review associate requests and payouts (5.2).
- Check banners and the scrolling notice are still current.


EVERY MONTH:

- Download a backup and keep it safely (8.3).
- Review promo codes that have ended and switch them off.
- Check Google Analytics for visitors, popular pages and busy days.
- Make sure the certificates and legal pages are up to date.


11. COMMON PROBLEMS
===================

* A customer says they paid but the order is still "Awaiting verification"
    What to do: Check your bank or UPI statement for that amount and
    reference, then Approve or Reject
* A promo code is refused
    What to do: Read the message shown. Check the minimum order, the
    services it is valid for, the dates and the per-customer limit in Promo
    codes
* Customers cannot choose a consultation time
    What to do: Add slots in Appointment slots (4.5)
* I changed something but the website still shows the old version
    What to do: Refresh the page (press Ctrl and F5 on a computer). Changes
    in Website settings are immediate
* The map shows the wrong place
    What to do: Business settings, Location Map. Check the latitude and
    longitude, or paste a fresh Google embed code
* A Kundli, Matching or Panchang page is empty
    What to do: The outside tool may be down. Try again later, or switch the
    page off in Website settings until it returns
* I forgot my password
    What to do: Contact the developer to reset it
* I deleted something by mistake
    What to do: Contact the developer at once. Downloaded backups can help
* Mobile OTP is not arriving
    What to do: The SMS service (MSG91) must be set up with its keys and
    template. Until then use email and password or Google login


12. DO AND DO NOT
=================

Do:

- Check payments in your own bank or UPI before approving.
- Fill both Hindi and English text.
- Keep slots, banners and the scrolling notice up to date.
- Download a backup every month.
- Log out when you finish.

Do not:

- Share the admin password or the API keys.
- Approve a payment without checking the money.
- Delete services or plans that already have orders. Deactivate them.
- Change legal text without the lawyer's approval.
- Upload very large pictures.


13. GETTING HELP
================

For technical help, restore from backup, or new features, contact the
developer. Please have ready: what you were doing, what you expected, what
happened, and the date and time. A screenshot helps.
