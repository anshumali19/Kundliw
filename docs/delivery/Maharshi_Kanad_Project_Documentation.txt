1. INTRODUCTION
===============


1.1 Purpose of this document
----------------------------

This document describes the Maharshi Kanad Jyotish Kendra web platform: what
it does, how it is built, how it is run and how it is kept safe. It is the
main handover document. A separate manual, "Admin Panel Manual", explains
day-to-day use for the Kendra's staff.


1.2 The project in one paragraph
--------------------------------

The platform lets visitors learn about the Kendra's Vedic astrology services,
see the day's Panchang, use free Kundli and Kundli-matching tools, book a
service online, pay by UPI or bank transfer, upload their Kundli, and follow
the progress of their order. The Kendra manages everything through a private
Admin Panel without touching code. An associate (referral partner) programme
lets partners earn commission on bookings they bring. The site works in Hindi
(default) and English and is designed to work well on phones.


1.3 Who uses it
---------------

* Visitor
    What they can do: Browse services, Panchang, tools, blog, gallery,
    certificates; contact the Kendra; chat with the assistant
* User (customer)
    What they can do: Everything a visitor can, plus book and pay, upload
    files, track orders, download invoices
* Associate
    What they can do: Share a referral link, see referrals and commission,
    request payouts
* Super Admin
    What they can do: Full control of content, orders, payments, settings,
    associates, backups


2. FEATURE SUMMARY
==================


2.1 Public website
------------------

- Home page with a one-line bar showing time, day, date, paksha, tithi,
  lunar month and Vikram Samvat (calculated daily by the site).
- Services and plans, service detail pages, Other services.
- Panchang, Kundli and Kundli-matching pages using AstroSage's free tools
  embedded in the page, each switchable on or off by the admin.
- Prashna Kundli request form.
- Offers page, certificates (GST, MSME, Trademark, ISO) with view, download
  and print.
- Blog (articles), gallery, reviews, FAQ, founder page, help, contact page
  with map and enquiry form.
- Social media links with icons (YouTube, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram,
  Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Telegram) controlled by the admin.
- Floating Call, WhatsApp, AI and Chat buttons (two on each side) that slide
  away while scrolling.
- AI-assisted chat widget with admin-managed answers.
- Mobile bottom bar (Home, Menu, Services, Booking, Offers, Account) and a
  menu sheet.
- Scrolling notice strip that visitors can pause and close.


2.2 Booking and payment
-----------------------

1. The customer chooses a service and plan and signs in.
2. Birth details are entered (and a partner's details for matching).
3. Plans that include a consultation call require a free appointment slot.
4. A promo code may be applied; the server checks minimum order, services,
  dates, usage limits and the customer's own usage.
5. The price is computed on the server (never trusted from the browser) with
  GST.
6. The customer pays by the Kendra's UPI, QR or bank details and submits the
  reference (and optional screenshot).
7. The admin verifies the payment, which creates the invoice; the admin then
  moves the order through its stages.
8. The customer tracks the order, receives files in their vault and
  downloads the invoice.

Online card or UPI payment through Razorpay is prepared on the server side but
needs the Kendra's Razorpay account and keys before it can be switched on.


2.3 Customer account and vault
------------------------------

- Sign in by mobile OTP, email and password, or Google.
- A private vault where the customer uploads their Kundli (PDF, images,
  video, audio, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text; up to 4 MB). A preview appears
  beside the upload button; Word and Excel files download.
- Orders list, invoice download, logout.


2.4 Associate programme
-----------------------

Registration with approval (or automatic approval), a personal referral link
and QR, commission rules per service or plan, personal commission overrides, a
commission life cycle (Pending, Earned, Paid, Cancelled), payout details and
payout requests with a minimum amount.


2.5 Admin Panel
---------------

A grouped sidebar with: Dashboard; Orders, Services and plans, Other services,
Appointment slots, Promo codes, Enquiries; Users, Associates, Customer files;
Website settings (15 sections), Banners, Scrolling notice, Pages and legal,
Articles, Gallery, Reviews, FAQ, Founder, Certificates, Translations, Chatbot;
Business settings, API keys and Analytics, Backups. See the Admin Panel
Manual.


3. ARCHITECTURE
===============


3.1 Overview
------------

     Visitor / Customer / Admin (browser, phone, Android WebView)
                    |
            HTTPS  v
       +----------------------------+
       |  Next.js application       |   Pages (server rendered), API routes,
       |  (hosted on Vercel)        |   proxy for locale and route guards
       +--+----------+----------+---+
          |          |          |
          v          v          v
      PostgreSQL   Vercel Blob   Outside services
      (Prisma)     (uploads)     Razorpay, MSG91, SMTP, Google sign-in,
                                 AstroSage embeds, Gemini AI, OpenStreetMap


3.2 Technology
--------------

* Framework
    Technology: Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript
* Styling
    Technology: Tailwind CSS 3 with a colour-token palette (saffron, maroon,
    yellow, red on cream)
* Languages
    Technology: next-intl (Hindi default, English) plus admin-editable
    translation overrides
* Database
    Technology: PostgreSQL through Prisma 5 (migrations in
    /prisma/migrations)
* Sign-in
    Technology: NextAuth 4 with JWT sessions: mobile OTP, email and
    password, Google
* Validation
    Technology: Zod schemas on every API input
* File storage
    Technology: Vercel Blob (private); local disk for development
* PDF
    Technology: pdfkit for invoices
* Tests
    Technology: Vitest (unit and API-level tests)
* Hosting
    Technology: Vercel (cron jobs configured in vercel.json)

3.4 Key design decisions
------------------------

- Prices, discounts and GST are always computed on the server from database
  records.
- Money is stored in paise (integers) to avoid rounding errors.
- Orders reserve appointment slots and promo-code usage for 30 minutes;
  unpaid reservations are released automatically.
- Everything the Kendra may want to change lives in the database (Website
  settings, Pages, Banners and so on), not in code.
- The Panchang is calculated from the Sun's and Moon's positions (Lahiri
  ayanamsha, Varanasi), so it needs no outside service.


4. DATA MODEL
=============

The database holds these main groups of records (Prisma models).

* Accounts
    Models: User, Account, VerificationToken, OtpVerification, RateLimit
* Catalogue
    Models: Service, Plan, OtherService, Offer
* Orders and payments
    Models: Order, OrderStatusEvent, Slot, Counter, ProcessedWebhookEvent
* Customer files
    Models: VaultFile
* Associates
    Models: AssociateProfile, CommissionRule, Commission, PayoutRequest
* Content
    Models: StaticPage, BlogPost, Banner, MarqueeItem, MediaGalleryItem,
    Testimonial, FaqItem, FounderProfile, Credential, TranslationOverride,
    ChatbotQA, ContactEnquiry
* Settings
    Models: AdminSettings (business, payment, map, associate rules,
    backups), SiteConfig (website settings sections)
* Operations
    Models: BackupRecord, PanchangCache

Migrations 0 to 10 are applied automatically at each deployment (the build
runs "prisma migrate deploy").


5. SECURITY
===========

* Passwords
    How it is protected: Stored only as bcrypt hashes
* Sessions
    How it is protected: Signed JWT cookies, HTTP-only, secure
* Roles
    How it is protected: Server-side checks on every admin, associate and
    customer API; admin pages re-check the role in the database
* Cross-site requests
    How it is protected: Origin check (CSRF protection) on every
    state-changing API
* Brute force
    How it is protected: Database-backed rate limits on login, OTP, coupon
    checks, uploads, tracking; admin lockout after repeated failures
* Input
    How it is protected: Zod validation on all input; links are limited to
    safe web addresses
* Uploads
    How it is protected: File type is verified from the file's actual
    content (not its name); executable, HTML and SVG files are refused; size
    limits; files are private and served only to the owner or admin with
    safe headers
* Secrets
    How it is protected: Third-party keys saved in the admin panel are
    encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) and never shown or exported; backups
    exclude secrets and password hashes
* Payments
    How it is protected: Amounts come from the server; webhooks are
    signature-checked and processed once
* Headers
    How it is protected: HSTS, no framing of the site, no content-type
    sniffing, strict referrer policy
* Privacy
    How it is protected: Customer files and details are visible only to the
    customer and the admin; the legal pages describe use of data
* Backups
    How it is protected: Automatic daily, weekly and monthly copies stored
    in the database; manual and downloadable copies

    Purpose: Session signing key and the public address of the site

    Purpose: Key used to encrypt API keys saved in the admin panel

    Purpose: Password for the first admin account
* CRON_SECRET
    Purpose: Authorises the scheduled jobs

    Purpose: File storage (set automatically by Vercel Blob)
* GEMINI_API_KEY
    Purpose: AI chat
* RAZORPAY_KEY_ID, RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET, RAZORPAY_WEBHOOK_SECRET
    Purpose: Online payment
* MSG91_AUTH_KEY (and sender and template)
    Purpose: OTP SMS
* SMTP_HOST, SMTP_PORT, SMTP_USER, SMTP_PASS, SMTP_FROM
    Purpose: Email
* GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID, GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET
    Purpose: Google sign-in
* NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_MEASUREMENT_ID
    Purpose: Google Analytics (or set it in the admin panel)
* DEMO_MODE
    Purpose: Developer switch for sample behaviour; keep it off for a fully

7. DEPLOYMENT AND OPERATIONS
============================


7.1 How a release happens
-------------------------

1. Code is committed to the main branch of the repository.
2. Vercel builds automatically: it applies database migrations, then builds
  the site.
3. If the build succeeds, the new version goes live in a few minutes. If it
  fails, the old version stays online.


7.2 Scheduled jobs
------------------

* expire-orders
    Time (UTC): 02:00 daily
    What it does: Releases unpaid reservations (slots and promo uses)
* backup
    Time (UTC): 20:30 daily (02:00 India)
    What it does: Creates a daily backup, plus weekly on Sundays and monthly
    on the 1st, and removes old copies


7.3 Health check
----------------

The address /api/health returns {"status":"ok"} when the site and database are
working. It can be used with a free uptime monitor.


7.4 Backups and restore
-----------------------

- Content and records are copied to the database daily and can be downloaded
  as JSON from Admin, Backups.
- Uploaded files live in file storage and should be backed up separately if
  they are critical.
- Restore is performed by the developer from a chosen backup.
- The hosting database provider also keeps its own point-in-time backups
  according to its plan.


7.5 Monitoring and maintenance
------------------------------

- Check the Vercel dashboard for failed builds and errors.
- Review Google Analytics monthly.
- Update dependencies and apply security patches every quarter.
- Renew the domain yearly.

11. GLOSSARY
============

* Panchang
    Meaning: The Hindu almanac: tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana and timings
* Tithi, Paksha
    Meaning: Lunar day and the bright (Shukla) or dark (Krishna) half of the
    month
* Vikram Samvat
    Meaning: The Hindu calendar year count
* Kundli, Janampatri
    Meaning: Birth chart
* Prashna Kundli
    Meaning: Chart cast for the moment a question is asked
* Vault
    Meaning: The customer's private file area
* Slot
    Meaning: A bookable time for a consultation call
* Associate
    Meaning: A referral partner who earns commission
* UTR
    Meaning: The reference number of a bank or UPI payment
* GST
    Meaning: Goods and Services Tax
* Webhook
    Meaning: An automatic message from the payment provider confirming a
    payment
* Migration
    Meaning: A versioned change to the database structure
